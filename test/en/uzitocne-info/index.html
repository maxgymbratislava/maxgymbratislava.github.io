USEFUL INFO

Before your visit, pay attention to these instructions and notes.

RESERVATION

Book through our booking page. Choose MaxGym Pro or MaxGym Solo, a 75 or 150 minute session and an available slot. You will receive your booking confirmation by email.

Book at least 30 minutes in advance. You can cancel up to 24 hours before your session.

Online payments and automatic entry codes are not active yet. If your booking confirmation has not arrived, check your spam folder or email maxgym@maxgym.sk.

ARRIVAL

Find us at Klincová 37, 821 08 Bratislava-Ružinov.

Public transport, parking and navigation details are coming before opening.

Arrange access: Automatic unlocking is not active yet. Your booking confirmation does not unlock the doors. Before visiting, contact maxgym@maxgym.sk to arrange access.

MaxGym Pro: entrance directly from the street.

MaxGym Solo: entrance through a corridor and two doors in a row.

TRAINING

Return equipment to its place and leave the gym clean after your workout. Do not let anyone in who is not part of your reservation. Final hygiene and facility instructions will be published before opening.

LEAVE

When leaving, take all your belongings, close the windows and make sure the door locks behind you.

Check that the gym is properly locked!

We look forward to your visit!
