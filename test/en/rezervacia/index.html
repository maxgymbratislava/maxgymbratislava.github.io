RESERVATION

Choose your gym (MaxGym Pro or MaxGym Solo), a 75 or 150 minute session and an available slot. After confirmation, you will receive your entry PIN by email.

Book at least 30 minutes in advance. You can change or cancel your reservation free of charge no later than 24 hours before your session.

Your entry code opens only the doors of the gym you booked — make sure you choose the right one.

You can also open bookings on a separate page.
