EQUIPMENT

Dumbbells

Basic equipment for strength training, used for exercises targeting the upper and lower body. Different weights enable a gradual increase in load. Available at SoloGym: 1–10 kg / 12.5–45 kg.

Benefits: strength, muscle mass, stabilization and coordination.

Power rack

A robust structure designed for safe training of squats, deadlifts and other heavy-weight exercises, with adjustable safety stops.

Benefits: increased strength, safety with heavy weights.

Olympic barbell

A standard 2.2 m barbell with a 28–32 mm grip diameter, intended for high loads — deadlifts, bench presses, squats.

Benefits: strength and muscle development, universal use.

EZ bar

Its curved shape makes gripping easier and reduces wrist stress during exercises like biceps curls or French presses.

Benefits: less joint load, effective biceps and triceps work.

Dip bars

For dips, pull-ups and other bodyweight exercises targeting the triceps, shoulders and chest.

Benefits: upper body strength and stability.

Battle rope

A great tool for training dynamics, strength and endurance — rope waves engage the whole body and boost fitness.

Benefits: cardiovascular fitness, strength and endurance.

Kettlebell

A ball-shaped weight with a handle for exercises improving strength, explosiveness and stability — swings, Turkish get-ups and more.

Benefits: strength, stability, coordination, complex training.

SkiErg

Simulates the motion of cross-country skiing — ideal for cardio and upper body strengthening.

Benefits: endurance, full body workout.

Airbike

A combination of stationary bike and resistance trainer using air resistance — the faster you pedal, the greater the resistance. Great for interval training.

Benefits: fitness, calorie burn, heart strengthening.

Plyo box

Equipment for jump exercises like box jumps, improving explosiveness and coordination.

Benefits: explosiveness, lower body speed and strength.

BOSU

A hemispherical unstable platform for balance exercises, coordination and muscle stabilization.

Benefits: balance, stability, core strength.

Resistance bands

Elastic bands with varying resistance levels — great for warm-ups, rehabilitation and muscle strengthening.

Benefits: flexible training, mobility, muscle activation.

Weight sled

For pushing or pulling loaded weights across the floor — develops strength, endurance and dynamics.

Benefits: explosiveness, lower body strength and endurance.

Punching bag

Ideal for training strength, speed and punching technique — boxing, kickboxing or MMA.

Benefits: strength, endurance, technique, coordination.

Pull-up bar

A classic tool for bodyweight training — pull-ups and other upper body exercises.

Benefits: back, shoulder and biceps strength.

Facilities

Bluetooth Soundbar — press the big black button next to the entrance to put it into pairing mode. A shower and toilet with all necessary accessories are available. SoloGym WiFi — password available in the gym.

Benefits: your own playlist, complete comfort.
