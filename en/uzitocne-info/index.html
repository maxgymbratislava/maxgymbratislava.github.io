USEFUL INFO

Before your visit, pay attention to these instructions and notes.

RESERVATION

Book anytime through our booking page. Choose MaxGym Pro or MaxGym Solo — your entry code only works for the gym you booked. The reservation is valid after payment.

Reservations must be made at least 30 minutes before the desired time. Cancellations with a refund are possible up to 24 hours before the start of the session.

If your access code has not arrived after payment, check your spam folder. If it is not there either, email us at maxgymbratislava@gmail.com.

ARRIVAL

Find us at Klincová 37, 821 08 Bratislava-Ružinov.

Public transport, parking and navigation details are coming before opening.

Entry code: Use your unique 6-digit entry code, sent by a separate email after your reservation is confirmed. Enter it on the keypad by the door. The code is generated individually for every reservation and is only valid for its duration.

TRAINING

The gyms are regularly cleaned and disinfected. Please disinfect the equipment after use and place a towel under you. A camera monitors the entrance; do not let anyone in who is not part of your reservation.

LEAVE

When leaving, take all your belongings, close the windows and make sure the door locks behind you.

Check that the gym is properly locked!
