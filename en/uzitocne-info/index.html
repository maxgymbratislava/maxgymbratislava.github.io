USEFUL INFO

Before your visit, pay attention to these instructions and notes.

RESERVATION

Book through our booking page. Choose MaxGym Pro or MaxGym Solo — your entry code is intended only for the gym you booked. Your reservation is confirmed once you receive the confirmation email.

Available slots, required booking notice, cancellation terms and any refund conditions are shown in the booking system before confirmation.

If your access code has not arrived after confirmation, check your spam folder. If it is not there either, email us at maxgym@maxgym.sk.

ARRIVAL

Find us at Klincová 37, 821 08 Bratislava-Ružinov.

Public transport, parking and navigation details are coming before opening.

Entry code: Use your unique 6-digit entry code, sent by a separate email after your reservation is confirmed. Enter it on the keypad by the door. The code is generated individually for each reservation; do not share it with anyone outside your booking.

MaxGym Pro: entrance directly from the street — enter your code on the keypad by the door.

MaxGym Solo: the entrance leads through a corridor, i.e. two doors in a row — enter the same code first on the outer door keypad and then on the gym door keypad.

TRAINING

Return equipment to its place and leave the gym clean after your workout. Do not let anyone in who is not part of your reservation. Final hygiene and facility instructions will be published before opening.

LEAVE

When leaving, take all your belongings, close the windows and make sure the door locks behind you.

Check that the gym is properly locked!

We look forward to your visit!
