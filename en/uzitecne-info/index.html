USEFUL INFO

Before your visit, pay attention to these instructions and notes.

RESERVATION

How to make a reservation

You can book the gym space anytime through our booking page. The reservation will only be valid after payment.

If you suspect your access code hasn't arrived after payment, please check your spam folder. If it's not there either, call +420 724 357 013.

Time restrictions

Reservations must be made at least 30 minutes before the desired time.

Cancellation policy

Cancellations with a refund are possible up to 24 hours before the start of the reserved session.

Group reservations

For larger groups (more than 3 people), an additional fee applies. See the price list.

ARRIVAL — how to get to us

On foot: We are just a 5-minute walk from the main train station.

Public transport: The Vlhká stop is only 20 meters from the gym. You can reach us by tram, trolleybus, or bus.

By car: Free parking is available at three designated spots right next to the gym.

Campus

Navigation: Upon arrival at the address, follow the banners leading you to the gym entrance.

Parking: Park only in the designated spots.

Night entry, weekends and holidays: The main gate from the street closes at night (approximately 19:00–6:00) and remains closed on weekends and holidays. To open it, use the code sent to your email and confirm with the asterisk (*).

Access code

Entry code: The gym doors automatically lock after 30 seconds. To enter, use your unique 6-digit entry code, which will be sent to you in a separate email after your reservation is confirmed. Enter the code on the keypad by the door. Please wait 20 seconds for the door to unlock.

Code validity: Each code is generated individually for every reservation and is only valid for its duration.

If your code hasn't arrived, please also check your spam folder. If it's not there either, call +420 724 357 013.

TRAINING

Cleanliness

The gym facilities are regularly cleaned and disinfected. Disinfectants are available throughout the gym — please disinfect the equipment after use and place a towel under you. Wipes and disinfectants are provided in the bathroom.

Safety

CCTV system: The camera monitors the entrance to the gym. Only the gym's security personnel have access to the footage. Recordings are stored for 7 days.

Entry rules: Do not let anyone into the gym who is not part of your reservation. Other visitors have their own codes.

Issue resolution: If you encounter any issues with the lock, equipment, or have other concerns, immediately call +420 724 357 013.

LEAVE

When leaving, press the button on the lock in the middle or unlock it manually. The lock will automatically engage after 30 seconds, and you can safely exit the gym.

Check that the gym is properly locked!
